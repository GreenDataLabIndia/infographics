How to read this dashboard

Active fire detection: a heat anomaly seen by VIIRS satellites (Suomi-NPP, NOAA-20, NOAA-21, 375 m) from NASA FIRMS. Each satellite pass counts separately, so one fire seen twice counts twice. FRP (fire radiative power, MW) shows intensity; “large” means FRP ≥ 30 MW. Dates are UTC acquisition dates. Surge = at least 10 detections and at least 3× the previous period. Rabi = wheat-residue season, Kharif = paddy-residue season. Detections are not the same as burnt area.
